How it works

Setup, once. Add a key under AI provider, put your handle under Your voice, save. Then open your profile on X and press Learn my voice. It scrolls your Posts and Replies tabs, keeping only things you wrote. Replies are weighted higher because that's what it's writing.

Every day. Open a post (or hit reply on one in your feed). Under the reply box press ✨ Suggest replies. Pick one with Use, edit if you like, send. Nothing is ever posted automatically.

Tuning. If the suggestions miss your voice, the fastest fix is a line or two in Style notes – the chips there are generated from your own samples. You can also nudge a single run with the Shorter / Funnier / More direct buttons under the suggestions.

Privacy. Keys and samples live in this browser's extension storage only. Each suggestion sends the post, a little thread context, your draft and your voice samples to the provider you chose – nowhere else.

X changes its page structure now and then. If the button disappears one day, the extension needs a small update to its selectors.
