How it works

Full Autopilot runs automatically in the background on your schedule, scanning your configured feeds, drafting replies, and scheduling them across your spread window.

You can also open the 🤖 Autopilot & Queue drawer on any X feed page and press ⚡ Auto-Scan & Schedule for instant one-click batch scheduling, or Scan for review if you want to edit specific drafts first.

How it works

1. Zero-touch setup. Add an API key under AI provider and save. When you open X (or enter your handle under Your voice), the extension automatically detects your handle, opens your profile in the background to learn your Posts & Replies, runs AI voice analysis, and applies your personal style rules.

2. Full background Autopilot. With Autopilot enabled, the extension periodically opens your Home feed (and any custom lists, search queries, or profiles you've added), scrolls to find fresh posts, generates replies in your voice, and schedules them across randomized times within your active hours and daily cap.

3. Smart pacing. Every scheduled reply opens briefly in a background tab at its due time, pauses naturally, inserts the reply, clicks Reply, verifies X's confirmation, and closes the tab.

4. On-page controls. On any X page you can open the 🤖 Autopilot & Queue drawer to pause/resume Autopilot, trigger an immediate scan, or review upcoming scheduled replies. When viewing an individual post, reply suggestions appear automatically under the reply box.

Privacy. Keys and samples live in this browser's extension storage only. Nothing is sent anywhere except directly to your chosen AI provider.
